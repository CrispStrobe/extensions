Licences of the MakeCode extensions ported into this gallery
==========================================================

The five extensions below are Scratch-native ports derived from MIT-licensed
MakeCode (PXT) packages. Each extension file repeats its upstream notice in its
header; the full licence text of each upstream repository, exactly as it stands
at the pinned commit, is reproduced here. The ports themselves are offered
under the same MIT licence (SPDX: MIT).

Sources and commits are those vendored in CrispStrobe/brickwright-lite
(overlay/scratch-gui/src/lib/bw-makecode/extensions-vendored.js).
Note that arcade-sprite-util and pxt-status-bar come from Joey Wunderlich's
repositories (jwunderl/...), not from the microsoft organisation.
